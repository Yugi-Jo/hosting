This channel is fast-paced and high energy. Every second should inform, entertain, or hook the viewer into the next moment. Dead air, filler words, slow pans, and awkward pauses are the enemy. Your job is to make sure they never get bored.

PRE-FLIGHT

Before You Start

Confirm you have all footage, assets, music, and any reference clips before touching the timeline.
A setup folder of resources will have been sent — check it first. It contains preferred audio tracks and a library of Premiere Pro presets. Use them as directed in the outline.
Epidemic Sound tracks are also approved. Need a specific track downloaded? Ask before proceeding.
Green screen footage of Yugi Jo will be sent pre-synced and mostly cut. If it hasn't been cut yet — start there first.
STEP 01

Rough Cut

Watch through all footage at least once before making a single cut. Use the Green Screen preset to key Yugi Jo's footage. Keep energy consistent throughout — flat delivery should be flagged, not automatically scrapped.

Cut Every Filler Word
  • "um" / "uh"
  • "like" / "you know"
  • "so" / "basically"
  • Any dead air over 0.5 seconds
Cut Without Thinking
  • Stumbles & re-dos
  • Long mid-sentence pauses
  • Off-camera noise / distractions
  • Repeated points — keep the best/cleanest
Flag — Don't Cut
  • Slow content that may be important
  • Segments you're unsure about
  • Off performance with no better take
Pacing Rules
  • Jump cuts = energy — use them freely
  • Flag any section 45+ sec with no visual break
  • Always use the cleanest take available
STEP 02

B-Roll & Clips

FORMAT

B-roll is overlayed over the top of the backdrop at 90 scale or 80 scale and given the preset PNG drop shadow. Apply this consistently to every B-roll clip.

Approved Sources

  • Original footage shot for video
  • Screen recordings / demos
  • Approved stock footage sites
  • Archival / reference clips

Never Use

  • AI-generated footage
  • Watermarked content
  • Unlicensed audio under clips
  • Any unapproved stock site
Cut to B-roll every time a specific thing, place, person, or concept is mentioned.
No more than 30–45 seconds of unbroken A-roll — use B-roll to break it up.
Trim B-roll tight. Cut back to A-roll the moment the point is made.
B-roll energy must match narration energy at all times.
NEVER

No AI-generated footage. No watermarked content. Not ever — regardless of context or reason.

STEP 03

Music & Sound

Music Rules
  • Never overpowers the voice
  • High-BPM for intro / transitions
  • Lower energy under informational sections
  • Always fade out — never hard cut
SFX Rules (Optional)
  • Punctuate jokes, transitions, text pop-ins
  • Keep subtle — less is more
  • Be consistent: same graphic = same sound
  • SFX is optional per video, not mandatory
STEP 04

Transitions

HOW TO

Pre-made transitions are included in the setup folder. Add the clip to the timeline, then set its blending mode to Screen. Get creative — use what fits the moment.

STEP 05

Graphics, Text & Captions

Text is for emphasis only — don't caption everything that's said.
Keep text short: 5 words or fewer where possible.
Font: Montserrat. Maintain consistent font and colour throughout the video.
Captions: bold text with a subtle dark background or outline for readability. Max 2 lines on screen. Break at natural speech pauses. Spoken word only — do not caption music or SFX.
Captions are optional per video. If used: auto-generate as a base, then manually correct all errors.
STEP 06

Colour Grading

Apply a consistent LUT or grade across all B-roll in a video. Do not mix grades. If the grade looks too heavy on certain footage, tone it down — but keep it consistent throughout. Colour grading is also done for copyright protection purposes.

TIP

If B-roll is flagging copyright issues at upload, add a 70–80% opacity logo watermark over the clip. See the Fairy Tail videos for reference on how this should look.

STEP 08

Copyright

NEVER

Use audio from copyrighted shows, films, or broadcasts without altering it first. Never use music that isn't cleared, licensed, or royalty-free. Never use AI-generated or watermarked footage.

If a B-roll clip has copyrighted audio — mute it and replace with music or SFX before delivery.
Upload to your channel as Unlisted first to check for copyright strikes before sharing the draft.
Persistent B-roll copyright issues can be resolved with a 70–80% opacity logo watermark over the clip.
STEP 09

Review & Delivery

Draft Delivery
  • Export & upload to Google Drive
  • Include a timecoded .txt for all flags
  • Note anything you were unsure about
Revisions
  • Timecoded notes will be provided
  • Work back-to-front to keep timecodes accurate
  • Re-export as v02, v03 — never overwrite
  • Unclear note? Ask on Discord
Final Delivery
  • Final export uploaded to the specified Google Drive folder — not just the general Drive
